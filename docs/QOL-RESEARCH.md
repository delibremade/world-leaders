# QoL and gameplay research (F6, #38)

Research only. No gameplay code. Written 2026-10-10 against v0.72.0. Owner request: survey open-source strategy and sim repos (study-only where GPL/AGPL) and shipped grand-strategy, 4X and sim games for quality-of-life and mechanics that fit World Leaders, map each to our engine and shell, rank a top 10 with issue-ready acceptance criteria, note licences.

**Verdict:** the shell already has the right skeleton (outliner, why-popovers, confirm sheet, event cards, pause-reason chip). The three gaps that cost the owner the most on a phone are: (1) toasts that vanish, (2) a clock that keeps running while a sheet is open, (3) a single autosave with no export. Fix those first (items 1 to 4). Then timeline, difficulty and onboarding (5 to 7). Mechanics (8, 9) and automation (10) come after P4, as PLAYABLE-PLAN already says.

Effort scale: **S** one PR under ~300 lines with tests. **M** one PR, 300 to 800 lines, tests, 390px screens. **L** engine change or multi-PR, parity re-baseline, new invariants. Model split follows PLAYABLE-PLAN: Sonnet for UI and mechanical ports, Opus for engine changes with parity proofs.

## 2. Candidate catalog

Each candidate: what it is, who does it best (link), how it maps to our engine and shell, effort, recommendation.

### A. Time controls

**A1. Step mode: "Next month" button and auto-pause rules.** Time advances one month per tap while stepping; a per-event-class rule says whether an event pauses, toasts, or only feeds. Best: Paradox. Stellaris 4.0 lets every message be set to notification, toast, popup or auto-pause ([Stellaris dev diary 366](https://admin-forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/stellaris-dev-diary-366-announcing-stellaris-4-0.1726042), [Patch 3.8 notes: disabled, enabled, or auto-pause](https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/index.php?mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop&title=Patch_3.8)); Victoria 3 added pause-on-event with a "Paused by Event" message in 1.8 ([Patch 1.8](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Patch_1.8)). OpenXcom's geoscape has six time steps from 5 seconds to 1 day ([Controls (OpenXcom)](https://www.ufopaedia.org/index.php/Controls_(OpenXcom))) and its sibling UFO: Alien Invasion auto-pauses on any important message ([UFO: Alien Invasion](https://gamicus.fandom.com/wiki/UFO:_Alien_Invasion)). Rebel Inc, the closest phone peer, pauses on every decision pop-up ([Decisions, Rebel Inc wiki](https://rebelinc.wiki.gg/wiki/Decisions)). Maps to: the `setInterval` in `App.jsx:470` becomes a scheduler with modes `run | step`; `tick()` is already a single call; the HUD speed group gains a step button; pause rules are a `{eventClass: 'pause'|'toast'|'feed'}` map in UI settings. Effort **S** (M with per-class rules). Recommend: yes, after #36.

**A2. Pause while a sheet or confirm is open (phone).** Plague Inc pauses when the disease menu is open ([speedrun.com thread quoting the behaviour](https://www.speedrun.com/posts/n6woo)). Maps to: add `sheetOpen || confirmOpen` to the interval gate; chip label "Paused: sheet open". Effort **S**. Recommend: yes, fold into A1.

**A3. Keyboard: space toggles pause, 1 to 4 speed, N next month, Esc closes sheet.** Paradox and OpenXcom use number keys for speed ([Controls (OpenXcom)](https://www.ufopaedia.org/index.php/Controls_(OpenXcom)); [Victoria 3 keyboard shortcuts](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Keyboard_shortcuts)). #36 adds space. Effort **S**. Recommend: fold into #36 follow-up, desktop only.

**A4. Survivor-style "no pause" rule.** Frostpunk 2 Survivor mode disables pausing ([Escapist: Frostpunk 2 difficulty settings](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/all-frostpunk-2-difficulty-settings-explained/)). Maps to a game rule (see H3). Effort **S** once H3 exists. Recommend: optional rule, not default.

### B. Notification and alert queues

Best: Stellaris 4.0 message settings ([dev diary 366](https://admin-forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/stellaris-dev-diary-366-announcing-stellaris-4-0.1726042)); OpenTTD's per-type Off / Summary / Full ([Message settings](https://wiki.openttd.org/en/Archive/Manual/Settings/Message%20settings), [NewsSettings fields](https://docs.openttd.org/source/d5/d75/structNewsSettings)); Victoria 3's feed, popups and toasts as three delivery types with a message-settings button ([Vickypedia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Vickypedia), [User interface](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/index.php?title=User_interface&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)); RimWorld letters stacked on the right, colour by severity, with a jump option ([Events, RimWorld wiki](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Events)); FreeOrion's SitRep, a per-turn list whose entries link to the object ([V0.4 Quick Play Guide](https://www.freeorion.org/index.php/V0.4_Quick_Play_Guide)); Unciv's `Notification` carries a category (General, Trade, Diplomacy, Production, Units, War, Religion, Espionage, Cities) and a list of click actions executed round-robin ([Notification.kt](https://github.com/yairm210/Unciv/blob/master/core/src/com/unciv/logic/civilization/Notification.kt)); OpenRCT2 moved notification toggles into a tab of the Recent Messages window in 0.4.24 ([changelog](https://openrct2.org/changelog), [config.ini notifications section](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/wiki/Settings-in-config.ini)). Maps to: `fx.toast(msg)` becomes `fx.notify({class, sev, text, jump})` in the engine's `fx` seam (presentation stays outside `src/sim`); the existing `noticeCooldowns` stays the throttle; the outliner's jump targets are reused; feed persisted in the save (bounded, say 200 entries) so a reload keeps it; the HUD badge counts unread. **B2. Maps to: a pure selector `alerts(view) -> rows` next to `buildOutliner`, a new outliner group "Needs you", each row with the engine's why terms and a jump; a mute set in UI settings. Effort **S** to **M**. Recommend: yes, right after B1.

**B3. Gossip-style rival feed.** What rivals did this month (sanctioned X, deployed to Y, funded J-36), gated by intel coverage. Best: Civ VI gossip with diplomatic visibility levels and a Gossip Report ([Diplomatic Visibility and Gossip](https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Diplomatic_Visibility_and_Gossip_(Civ6))). Maps to: feed class `rival` fed from the rival phases in `tick.js`; needs I3 for gating. Effort **M**. Recommend: after B1 and I3.

### I. Mechanics that deepen existing systems

**I1. Crisis as a diplomatic play with backers (escalation, diplomacy).** An ultimatum or blockade becomes a timed play: opening moves, manoeuvring, countdown; third nations with an interest can be swayed by either side with offers; sympathy shifts with backers and demands; either side can back down and concede the primary goal. Best: Victoria 3 diplomatic plays ([Diplomatic play](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/index.php?title=Diplomatic_play&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)). Maps to: `ultimatum` and `confrontation` gain `phase`, `backers`, `offers`; bloc and pact data decide who has an interest; the Tier-2 escalation ladder (PLAYABLE-PLAN V2) renders the phases; rulings 1 and 3 untouched. Effort **L**. Recommend: yes, after P4; the single best deepening of V2.

**I2. World tension gate (escalation).** A global meter raised by aggressive acts (blockade, Tier-1 op, nuclear demonstration), decaying over time; thresholds unlock rival and ally behaviour (allies join sanctions above X, rivals accept back-channels below Y). Best: HoI4 world tension, generated per act, decays, gates actions by ideology ([World tension](https://hoi4.paradoxwikis.com/index.php?title=World_tension&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)); DEFCON counts down and unlocks weapons per level ([DEFCON (video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEFCON_(video_game))); Twilight Struggle, where DEFCON 1 ends the game and the player who caused it loses ([GMT living rules 2015](https://www.gmtgames.com/nnts/TS_Rules-2015.pdf)). Maps to: one scalar `worldTension` with an invariant 0..100, written where tension per rival is written, read by the AI response phases; shown in the HUD. Effort **M**. Recommend: yes, after I1 or inside it.

**I3. Intel levels and fog on rival internals (intel).** What you see of a rival (treasury, programs, deployments, tension drivers) depends on an intel level 0..100 built from embassies, stations, satellites and ops; ops have infiltration requirements and spend infiltration. Best: Stellaris Intelligence, intel 0..100 capped by infiltration, categories from none to full, operations with requirements and influence cost ([Intelligence](https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/Intelligence)); Civ VI diplomatic visibility levels ([Diplomatic Visibility and Gossip](https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Diplomatic_Visibility_and_Gossip_(Civ6))). Maps to: `intelLevel[rival]` written by a monthly system from `embassies`, `intelInfra`, `continuousOps`; the nation sheet and dossier (V7 Tier-2) blur fields below thresholds; invariant: never keyed by the player (ruling 6). Effort **L**. Recommend: yes, after P4. ROADMAP 007.

**I4. Leverage tokens (intel to diplomacy).** A successful op can yield a "hook" on a nation: one use, raises acceptance of a visit, pact or trade ask, or forces it; strong hooks reusable with a cooldown. Best: CK3 weak and strong hooks ([Hooks](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Hooks)). Maps to: `hooks[nation] = {kind, expires}`, spent by the diplomacy verbs; shown on the nation sheet. Effort **M**. Recommend: after I3.

**I5. Mineral market with price response (minerals).** Buying pushes the price up, selling down; export controls elsewhere move the world price; a flow view with 1, 12 and 60 month windows. Best: Offworld Trading Company, a single price moved by each buy and sell ([Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Offworld_Trading_Company), [designer notes](https://www.designer-notes.com/otc-designer-notes-2-free-markets/)); Factorio's production and consumption windows ([Production statistics](https://wiki.factorio.com/Production_statistics)); Victoria 3's investment pool as the model for #37 private capital ([Treasury](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treasury), [Building](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Building)). Maps to: `minerals.price[m]` with elasticity in `src/data/minerals.js`, D1 history for the windows. Effort **M**. Recommend: after #37 lands, since #37 changes who builds plants.

**I6. Force postures as rules of engagement and reinforce templates (forces).** C3 plus an explicit per-theater ROE (hold fire, escort only, engage runners) feeding the blockade and confrontation rolls. Best: Stellaris fleet templates ([Ship](https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/Ship)); Distant Worlds 2 fleet auto-response range policies ([Stellar update](https://www6.slitherine.com/news/distant-worlds-2-feature-stellar-update)). Maps to: `forcePosture` already exists per region; add a template and the reinforce verb. Effort **M**. Recommend: with C3.

**I7. Era timeline (technology).** Covered by D2.
